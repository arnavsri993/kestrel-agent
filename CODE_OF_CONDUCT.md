# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Kestrel is an owner-maintained development preview. Everyone who participates
in the project, its issue tracker, or its other project spaces is expected to
help make those spaces welcoming, respectful, and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful of different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and receiving constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it;
- communicating clearly about constraints, evidence, and uncertainty; and
- respecting privacy, security boundaries, and the project's contribution
  policy.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publishing private
information without permission, or attempting to bypass project security,
privacy, or approval boundaries. Unwelcome sexualized language or imagery and
other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe are also not
acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is
representing the project or its community. It also applies when conduct has a
substantial impact on the safety or ability of others to participate in those
spaces.

## Reporting Concerns

Please report unacceptable behavior privately to [@arnavsri993](https://github.com/arnavsri993)
or through the private channel that provided repository access. Do not include
passwords, tokens, credentials, private memory, or other sensitive data in a
report. For suspected security vulnerabilities, follow [the security
policy](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

The repository owner will review reports, protect the reporter's privacy where
possible, and determine an appropriate response. Reports made in good faith
will not result in retaliation.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may remove or edit comments, close issues, restrict
participation, or revoke repository access when necessary to address
unacceptable behavior. Decisions will be proportionate to the circumstances
and documented to the extent that doing so does not expose private information.
